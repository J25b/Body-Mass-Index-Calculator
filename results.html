Privacy First

Body IQ was built without accounts or ads, and keeps data collection to the bare minimum needed to know the tool is actually useful. Here's what that means in practice:

  • No account or sign-up required
  • No advertisements, ever
  • No cookies, fingerprinting, or third-party trackers
  • Your BMI, calorie, and recommendation calculations happen locally, in your browser, and stay there
  • We record anonymous, aggregate usage counts (like how many people visited, and how many BMI calculations were run) — never your name, measurements, goals, or results
  • Your personal results are never uploaded anywhere unless you choose to download or share them yourself

Medical Disclaimer

Body IQ is an educational wellness tool designed to provide general health information. BMI, calorie estimates, and personalized recommendations are general guidance only and are not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. BMI is only one indicator of health and may not accurately represent body composition for everyone, including athletes, pregnant individuals, older adults, or people with high muscle mass. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making significant changes to your diet, exercise routine, or lifestyle.
